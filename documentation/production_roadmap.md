# Future Production-Readiness Roadmap (Not Implemented in This Prototype)

1. Confirm source APIs/exports, scopes, authentication, permissions, rate limits and provider constraints for CRM, operations software and Google Sheets.
2. Build scheduled/incremental ingestion with retries, error queues, monitoring and data-freshness alerts.
3. Establish a governed canonical account/customer model and reconcile identifiers and unmatched records.
4. Select managed relational storage or a warehouse based on scale, reporting latency and ownership needs.
5. Agree metric definitions, ownership, financial treatment and automated reconciliation with business stakeholders.
6. Add role-based access for sales users, managers and administrators.
7. Implement encryption, secrets management, audit logs, backups, retention/deletion and privacy controls.
8. Test joins, KPI calculations, data freshness, access control and regressions.
9. Evaluate any future AI against curated examples; require source traceability, uncertainty disclosure, human review and safeguards against invented facts.
10. Add CI/CD, deployment monitoring, performance/cost controls, maintenance and support ownership.

These are roadmap considerations only; none should be represented as completed in this two-day prototype.
